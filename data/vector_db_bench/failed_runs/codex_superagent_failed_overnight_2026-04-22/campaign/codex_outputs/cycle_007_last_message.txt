**Changed**

Added [capture_request_shapes](</__modal/volumes/vo-WauxZXgTehLrBgTwgcOh2J/campaign/workspace/.meta_codex/tools/capture_request_shapes:1>) plus a README entry so future cycles can capture the official benchmark client’s raw reqwest HTTP shape locally. That confirmed plain `http://` search requests are very stable here: lower-case `content-type`, `accept`, `host`, `content-length`, with official compact search bodies in the `572..648` byte range.

I used that data to try a connection-local fixed-header search parser in `src/server.rs`. It stayed correct, but clean serial quick reruns regressed to `11440.96`, `11369.62`, and `11205.93` QPS, all at `1.0000` recall, so I reverted `src/server.rs` to the known-good mainline state. I recorded the result in [research_notes.md](</__modal/volumes/vo-WauxZXgTehLrBgTwgcOh2J/campaign/workspace/.meta_codex/research_notes.md:1>) and [campaign_journal.md](</__modal/volumes/vo-WauxZXgTehLrBgTwgcOh2J/campaign/workspace/.meta_codex/campaign_journal.md:1>). `build_project` passed after the restore.

**Score**

The kept workspace is back on the incumbent Rust path, so score expectations are unchanged. Best known valid full remains `11821.60` QPS at `1.0000` recall; the discarded cycle-7 parser branch was worse. I rebuilt after restore, but I did not rerun a full benchmark on the restored snapshot.
